🧪 Electrochemical Mechanism

Corrosion requires an electrolyte (water droplet) and oxygen. The iron pit acts as anode (oxidation), while the oxygen-rich edge acts as cathode. Electrons flow through the metal.

⚡ Half-Reactions

Anode: Fe → Fe²⁺ + 2e⁻
Cathode: O₂ + 2H₂O + 4e⁻ → 4OH⁻
Fe²⁺ + OH⁻ → Fe(OH)₂ → Fe₂O₃·H₂O (rust)

🔬 Visual cues

Brown particles = hydrated iron(III) oxide. Higher rate = faster rust particle generation. Reset clears rust layer.
