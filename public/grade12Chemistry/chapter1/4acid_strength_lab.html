📖 Learning objectives

Dissociation: Strong acids break apart completely. Weak acids exist mostly as molecules.

Ka & equilibrium: Larger Ka = more ions = stronger acid.

🔬 Interactive guide

💧 Strong acid (left beaker) ▼
HCl dissociates fully into H₃O⁺ and Cl⁻. All "H" protons are separated from anions → high conductivity.
⚖️ Weak acid (right beaker) ▼
HA ⇌ H⁺ + A⁻. Most molecules remain intact. Use slider to increase Ka → more dissociation and free protons.
📈 Ka & strength correlation ▼
Higher Kₐ means the equilibrium shifts right, producing more ions. This 3D simulation shows separated protons (blue) orbiting anions (green) when dissociated.

🧪 Chemistry notes

Strong acids (HCl, HNO₃) have Kₐ >> 1. Weak acids (acetic, carbonic) have small Kₐ. The slider adjusts relative strength of the weak acid side from ~10⁻⁶ to ~10⁻².
