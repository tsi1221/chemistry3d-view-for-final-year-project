⚛️ Ion Legend

Na⁺ (Sodium cation)

ions: 27
📐 Why alternate pattern? ▼
Rock salt structure: Na⁺ and Cl⁻ alternate in all three dimensions. Each Na⁺ is surrounded by 6 Cl⁻ (octahedral coordination). This maximizes electrostatic attraction and minimizes repulsion — the key to ionic crystal stability.
