📖 why 8? · octet rule explained ▼
✨ The octet rule: atoms are most stable when their outermost (valence) shell holds 8 electrons (like noble gases).
➜ Metals lose electrons → positive ions.
➜ Nonmetals gain electrons → negative ions.
