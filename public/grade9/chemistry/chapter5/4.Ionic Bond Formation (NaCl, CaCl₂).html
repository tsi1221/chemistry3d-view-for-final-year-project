📖 how ionic bonds form ▼
✨ Ionic bonding: Transfer of valence electrons from metal to nonmetal.
➜ Na → Na⁺ + e⁻ (loses 1 electron)
➜ Cl + e⁻ → Cl⁻ (gains 1 electron)
➜ Opposite charges attract → ionic lattice.
CaCl₂: Calcium loses 2 electrons → two chlorines gain one each.
