📘 Why does water dissolve salt? ▼
Polar water molecules surround Na⁺ and Cl⁻ ions, overcoming electrostatic lattice energy → dissociation. Free ions conduct electricity.
Oil (non-polar) cannot stabilize ions → crystal remains intact → no conductivity.
⚡ dissociation ↔ conductivity
