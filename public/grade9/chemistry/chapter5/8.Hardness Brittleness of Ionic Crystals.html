✨ crystal stable · alternating charges
📐 Why repulsion shatters? ▼
In an undisturbed lattice, each Na⁺ is surrounded by Cl⁻ (attraction). When mechanical stress shifts a plane, Na⁺-Na⁺ or Cl⁻-Cl⁻ come into contact → Coulomb repulsion (F ∝ 1/r²) → cracks propagate instantly. This experiment simulates layer displacement and the resulting electrostatic explosion.
