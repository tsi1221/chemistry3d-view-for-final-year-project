📖 why do ions form? · octet rule ▼
✨ Ion formation: Atoms gain or lose electrons to achieve a full valence shell (octet).
➜ Cation (+): loses electrons → more protons than electrons (e.g., Na⁺).
➜ Anion (−): gains electrons → extra negative charge (e.g., Cl⁻).
