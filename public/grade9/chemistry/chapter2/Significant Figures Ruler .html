📏 significant figures

📐 rules of sig figs
🔵 certain digits — reliably known from scale
🟠 uncertain digit — last digit, estimated
📏 more precise scale → more certain digits
💡 example: 42.35 cm has 4 sig figs
42.35 cm
📍 measurement 42.35
🎯 significant figures reflect measurement precision
• more certain digits = better instrument
• last digit is always estimated (uncertain)
• used in science, engineering, medicine
● certain (reliable) ● uncertain (estimated)
