⚡ Physics Principle (click) ▼

Magnetic sector MS: Ions with charge q, velocity v enter perpendicular magnetic field B. Lorentz force F = qvB provides centripetal acceleration: m·v²/r = qvB → r = mv/(qB).
➜ Heavier isotopes (higher m) have larger radius → less deflection → hit detector further down.
➜ Slider changes B field strength: higher B bends light isotopes more, adjusting which masses reach the detector.
